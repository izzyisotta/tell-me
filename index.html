a favour from Izzy

Tell me about it.

The thing I'm building needs to learn how people actually talk about their days. Voice-note me yours: a tap, then talk, whenever you've done a thing.

the ask

Five to ten voice notes over the next few weeks. One whenever you've done a thing.

  • A thing is a meal, a pub, a film, a play, a gig, a walk, a trip, a party, or a person you saw. Anything that happened. Tell me about it the way you'd tell a mate.
  • Say the specifics. Who was there, where you were, what you thought, whether you'd go back. Names are welcome, that's the whole point. Go easy on other people's secrets, though: if it's something they'd mind being written down by a stranger, leave it out.
  • Ramble. Thirty seconds is fine, three minutes is lovely. No polish, no structure, no do-overs. Even one is a gift.
on iphone · one minute, once

Get the shortcut. Then it's one tap.

It's an iPhone shortcut called Debrief. It lives right here on this page.

If this opened inside WhatsApp, tap the Safari icon (bottom right) to open it in Safari first, or the download won't land.

Get the shortcut

One tap here, then follow the lines that appear.

Get the shortcut
    1. Add it. Tap the green button above and follow the lines under it. It ends with you tapping Add Shortcut. Done once.
    2. Put it on your Home Screen. Open the Shortcuts app, hold Debrief → Details → Add to Home Screen → Add. Now it's an icon like any app. (Got an Action Button? Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → Debrief is even quicker. Or just say "Hey Siri, Debrief".)
    3. Tap. Talk. Tap to stop. The first time it'll ask about the microphone and about sending to me: tap Always Allow to both or it nags every time. When it's worked you get a little "Sent to Izzy" notification. It also keeps a copy in your Files (iCloud Drive → Shortcuts → Debrief), so if it grumbles about no signal on the Tube the recording's still there: WhatsApp it to me when you're up top.

    Not on iPhone? Then it's even simpler: WhatsApp me the voice notes. You know where I am.

    the small print, which is short

    Where your voice goes.

    Straight to a private store only I can get into. I'm the only person who listens. Each note is transcribed and run through AI models to test the thing I'm building; that is the entire use. The two services that do that are under contracts that forbid them training on anything I send.

    Names get swapped for made-up ones in anything I write up or show anyone. Nothing is ever published, posted, or played to anyone.

    Say the word and I delete yours, same day. Same goes if someone you mentioned wants out. No questions and no sulking.

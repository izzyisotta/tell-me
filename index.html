the ask

Voice-note me your life. A bit of it, anyway.

Over the next couple of weeks, whenever you've done a thing, tell me about it the way you'd tell a mate. A tap, then talk.

  • A thing is a meal, a pub, a film, a gig, a walk, a trip, a party, or a person you saw. Anything that happened.
  • Say the specifics. Who was there, where you were, what you thought, whether you'd go back. Names are welcome, that's the whole point. Go easy on other people's secrets, though: if it's something they'd mind being written down by a stranger, leave it out.
  • Ramble. Thirty seconds is fine, three minutes is lovely. No polish, no structure, no do-overs.
  • Five to ten of them would make my month. One is still a gift.

on iphone · one minute, once

Set it up once, then it's one tap.

  1. Type the word I sent you below and add the shortcut. It's called Tell Isabella.
  2. Put it somewhere you'll hit it. Action Button (Settings → Action Button → Shortcut) if you have one, or hold the shortcut and tap Add to Home Screen. Back-tap works too.
  3. Tap. Talk. Tap to stop. It sends itself to me and keeps a copy in your own Files, so nothing is lost if you're on the Tube.
